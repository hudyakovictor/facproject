4. Specific Risks (as enumerated in the TZ)
Risk area	Detail	Impact	Suggested mitigation
Pose leakage	Primary metrics correlate with pose_distance.	May flag normal pose changes as anomalies.	Include pose distance as covariate; adjust thresholds per pose bin.
Texture = era bias	Texture correlates with year of capture (r = 0.52).	Temporal trends mistaken for identity change.	Residualise texture against year; include era as covariate.
Calibration coverage	~46 frontal‑profile frames out of range.	Those pairs evaluated with extrapolated thresholds.	Mark as “out‑of‑calibration” and exclude/flag.
Point support bias	81 % pairs have < 20 points.	Low statistical power; metrics unstable.	Report point‑count per pair; down‑weight or bin by point count.
FDR on dependent data	Order‑statistic approximation may be inaccurate.	Actual false‑discovery proportion diverges.	Use permutation‑based FDR estimate.
Inflation factor x5.83	Origin unclear.	May over‑ or under‑correct.	Document derivation; test via bootstrap.
Threshold calibration	Pose gap, quality, chronology: hand‑picked or learned?	Reproducibility risk.	Record calibration set, hyper‑parameters, validation curve.
Multiple comparisons	5 561 pairs × 13 descriptor families.	Inflated type‑I error.	Apply hierarchical FDR or Bonferroni across families.
Status ≠ verdict	“coherent_jump_candidate” ≠ proof.	Misleading if reported as definitive.	Explicitly state evidential weight; avoid categorical language.
Confounders	Illumination, JPEG compression, camera age.	May create spurious anomalies.	Log these variables; test for interaction effects.
Temporal confounding	Natural aging vs. substitution.	Baseline may be absent/outdated.	Include age‑gradient model; provide “age‑adjusted” metrics.
